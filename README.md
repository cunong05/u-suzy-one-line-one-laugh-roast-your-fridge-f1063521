## Entry

One line roasting my fridge, for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) competition
**One Line, One Laugh: Roast Your Fridge**. The program writes exactly one
line of at most 200 characters to `/output/entry.txt` — nothing else.

> My fridge hums like a jet engine, guards three expired yogurts like treasure, and still can't keep the milk cold — the laziest employee in my kitchen, and its only job is to chill.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It prints the roast and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
